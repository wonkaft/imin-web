How imin makes money

Free to match and message. Revenue comes from optional semester passes for people who want more than the default pace.

Free, always

Matching, messaging, and the whole one-match-at-a-time experience is free for everyone — that's the core product, not a limited trial.

Semester Pass — $3.99

A one-time purchase that unlocks 3 concurrent matches instead of 1, valid through the end of that semester — a Fall Pass and a Spring Pass, each sold separately. It's not a subscription and doesn't auto-renew, which gives us a natural, recurring purchase moment twice a year for every active user without the friction of a subscription commitment.

Room to grow

Longer-term: premium tiers with more features, and eventually paid placement for local businesses in our meetup suggestions once we have real usage data to offer them.

Built to be safe, not just fun

A dating app built for real students needs real safety, not an afterthought.

Automatic moderation

Every photo and message is automatically screened for nudity, hate speech, and threats before it ever reaches another person.

Report & block

Reporting or blocking someone ends the match immediately, and reports are reviewed by our team within hours.

Zero tolerance

Harassment, threats, and abuse result in immediate, permanent removal — no warnings, no exceptions.

Verified students only

Every account is tied to a real college email, keeping the community small, trusted, and genuine.

Tech stack

What imin is actually built with.

App

React Native (Expo), built and deployed to the App Store through Expo EAS.

Backend

Supabase — Postgres, auth, file storage, realtime messaging, and Deno-based edge functions for custom server logic.

Payments

RevenueCat and Apple StoreKit handle the Semester Pass purchase and entitlement tracking.

Location

Google Places API powers the meetup suggestions between matched users.

Content safety (AI)

Sightengine's AI models automatically screen every photo and message for nudity, hate speech, and threats before it reaches another user.

Email

Resend handles transactional email — confirmations, password resets, and moderation alerts.

Built with AI

Claude Code (Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 5) has been used as an AI pair-programmer for the majority of the app's features, debugging, and infrastructure.
